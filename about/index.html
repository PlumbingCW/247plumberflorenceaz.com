How we work

We keep the process simple. You call, we ask what is happening and we tell you what to do first. A plumber comes out, finds the cause and gives you a price before starting.

  • Calls are answered by phone, not forms or chat.
  • Prices are given before work begins.
  • We explain the problem and the options.
  • We test the repair before leaving.

What we cover

We handle drains, sewer lines, water heaters, leaks, fixtures, repiping, installs, commercial plumbing and water treatment. See the full list of services for details on each.

We serve Florence, Anthem at Merrill Ranch, Coolidge and San Tan Valley. The service area page has the rest.

Why we link to official sources

Our guides and service pages cite utility reports, county notices and federal agencies when they state a fact about water, permits or rules. If a source does not publish a number, we say so and do not guess.

What happens when you call

A dispatcher answers, asks for your address and listens to what is happening. If water is flowing, the first instruction is to close the main valve. If sewage is backing up, it is to stop using water in the house.

  1. 1Answer.A person picks up the dispatch line, any hour of the day.
  2. 2Triage.We decide whether the problem is an emergency or can wait for a scheduled visit.
  3. 3Guidance.You get simple steps to limit damage until the plumber arrives.
  4. 4Visit.The plumber finds the cause and explains the options.
  5. 5Price and repair.You approve a price before the work starts.

Our approach to honest advice

Some problems do not need a big repair. A running toilet may need a cheap flapper, and a slow drain may need a strainer and a better habit. We say so. When a bigger job is needed, we explain why and show the evidence, such as camera footage or a pressure reading.

We do not promise arrival times we cannot keep, and we do not quote prices over the phone for problems we have not seen. We explain what moves the price and give a number once we know the job.

Local knowledge that shapes the work

Florence is the Pinal County seat on the south bank of the Gila River. The Florence Townsite Historic District, listed in 1982, holds more than 25 buildings, and older construction means older plumbing. Newer areas such as Anthem at Merrill Ranch have different issues, from builder-grade fixtures to scale in hard groundwater.

Water suppliers differ across our area, and so do permit offices. Our location pages for Coolidge and San Tan Valley explain which apply to you.

Tips for getting the most from any plumbing visit

A little preparation helps a plumber work faster and helps you control the cost.

  • Know where your main water shutoff is and make sure it turns.
  • Clear a path to the work area and move valuables.
  • Write down what you noticed and when it started.
  • Photograph the problem before and after.
  • Ask what the price includes and what could change it.

What we do not do

We do not use forms, chat bots or booking portals. We do not quote a final price for a problem we have not seen. We do not claim response times we cannot keep, and we do not recommend work the evidence does not support.

If a job is outside what we handle, such as large new construction or medical gas piping, we say so and suggest you look for a specialist. That honesty costs us a few jobs and saves everyone time.

Service for owners and renters

Homeowners, renters, landlords and property managers all call us. If you rent, tell your landlord about a leak right away, because many leases require notice. We can provide a written description of what we found for the landlord or the insurer, which keeps the claim simple.

Codes and standards we follow

We work to the plumbing code adopted where the job sits, usually based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) or the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and to the manufacturer's instructions for the equipment we install. This page was written by the Florence 24/7 Plumbing team.
