Galvanized, copper, CPVC and PEX

Each material has strengths and limits, and your home's condition and budget set the choice.

Water quality, hardness and pipe life

Water chemistry affects pipe life. Hard water leaves scale that narrows pipe, and aggressive water can eat copper. The Town of Florence Consumer Confidence Report for 2025 does not list a hardness figure, so test your tap or ask your utility for its annual report. EPCOR's San Tan system report lists hardness of 15 to 18 grains per gallon.

Whatever the pipe, knowing your water helps choose between a softener, a filter or no treatment at all. See water softener and filtration for the next step.

Sources: Town of Florence 2025 Consumer Confidence Report, EPCOR 2025 San Tan water quality report.

How a repipe works and what to expect in the house

A repipe is a project, so planning matters.

  1. 1Survey.We trace the existing lines and decide where the new ones will run.
  2. 2Access.We open small sections of drywall or use attic and crawl space routes to limit damage.
  3. 3Install.New pipes go in with new shutoffs, and fixtures are reconnected.
  4. 4Test.We pressure test the system before closing walls.
  5. 5Patch.Access holes are patched to rough finish. Texture and paint are a separate trade.

Water is usually off during the day while we work. We plan around your schedule and tell you in advance.

Whole-house or partial repipe

Sometimes only part of a system needs replacement. A single branch to a bathroom may be the problem. A full repipe is a stronger fix when the whole system shows wear. We explain the trade-off, and you pick the plan.

A phased repipe spreads cost across stages, but it means more disruption overall. We discuss that honestly, and we do not recommend a full repipe when tests do not support it.

Permits and inspection

Repiping is usually a permitted job. Ask the building department that covers your address. In the Town of Florence that is Community Development at 224 W. 20th Street, 520-868-7575. Pinal County Building Safety at 85 N Florence Street handles unincorporated areas, and for San Tan Valley the County handles building permits and inspections under its agreement with the Town.

Sources: Town of Florence, Pinal County.
