When a burst pipe is an emergency and how to stop the water

If water is spraying, pouring from a ceiling or flowing along a floor, every minute adds damage. Stopping the flow is the single most useful thing you can do.

  1. 1Find the main shutoff.It is usually near the water meter, at a hose bib on the front or side of the house, or inside the garage.
  2. 2Turn it off and open a tap.Close the valve fully, then open one faucet low in the house to relieve pressure.
  3. 3Stay safe around electricity.Keep away from outlets and cords in wet areas, and switch off breakers only if the panel is dry.
  4. 4Call the 24/7 line.Tell us where the water is and what pipe you think burst.

If you cannot find or close the main valve, call us anyway. The line is staffed around the clock and the plumber can close the supply at the meter if needed.

Why pipes burst in the desert

Freezing gets the headlines elsewhere, but burst pipes in Florence come from several causes. Winter nights do dip below freezing now and then, and exposed hose bibs, irrigation lines and pipes in exterior walls or attics can freeze. Water pressure that runs too high strains joints and fittings.

Age and corrosion do their work over the years. Copper can develop pinholes, galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside and plastic fittings crack. Exposed PVC and irrigation lines turn brittle in summer sun and can split without warning.

A pressure regulator that has failed can raise house pressure over time. We test pressure when we find one burst, since it often explains why a pipe let go.

Supply valve and fixture connection failures

Not every emergency is a pipe. A burst supply line under a sink, a failed toilet shutoff or a corroded fixture valve can put out as much water as a pipe break. When a valve will not close, we replace it and test the whole fixture. See faucet repair and toilet repair for those faults.

What to document for insurance

Take photos and video of the burst and the water damage before anything moves. Keep the damaged section of pipe if you can. Make a list of damaged belongings and write down when you found the leak.

Policies differ. Many cover sudden damage from a burst pipe but not the repair of the pipe itself. A claim may also require that you stop the water and begin repairs promptly. We stop the leak and repair the pipe but do not handle drying and rebuilding, so ask your insurer for a restoration company if you need one.

When repeated bursts mean the system is wearing out

One burst in a sound system is a repair. Two or three in a short time are a warning. If the camera and pressure tests show widespread corrosion, replacing the whole supply system can cost less than repairing each break. We explain both paths on the repiping page and let you decide.
