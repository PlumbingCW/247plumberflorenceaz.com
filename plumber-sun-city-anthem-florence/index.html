About Sun City Anthem at Merrill Ranch

A 55+ community guide describes Sun City Anthem as a Del Webb and Pulte community with about 3,800 planned homes and construction beginning in 2006. It lists single-family homes of roughly 1,100 to more than 2,800 square feet. The guide says the community is not gated.

Residents also use the wider Anthem at Merrill Ranch amenities, including a golf course and a water park. The all-ages Parkside neighborhoods share the same master plan, and we cover them on our page about Anthem at Merrill Ranch.

A Town council approval reported in 2023 covered Unit 30B, a final plat of more than 42 acres and 159 residential lots in the Sun City portion, generally east of Hunt Highway and south of Franklin Road.

Sources: 55places community guide, Homes.com neighborhood guide, and Rose Law Group Reporter coverage of the Florence Town Council.

Water heaters and the comfort of an easy repair

Most homes in the community are single-level, so the water heater is usually in the garage or a utility closet that is easy to reach. A tank does not last forever, and a leaking or noisy tank deserves a look before it floods a floor. See water heater repair and water heater replacement.

If you are comparing options, a tankless water heater changes the space and the gas or electric needs, so ask before you commit.

What to do if you are away from home

Many 55+ residents travel for weeks at a time. These steps reduce the risk of a leak while the house is empty.

  • Close the main water valve before a long trip.
  • Ask a neighbor with a key to check the house during long absences.
  • Turn the water heater to its vacation setting or off, following the manual.
  • Replace old washing machine hoses before you leave. See leak detection if you find damp areas.

Which water utility to call

The Town of Florence says on its start-service page that it does not provide water service in Anthem at Merrill Ranch. The Arizona Department of Environmental Quality lists the Anthem area of Florence among EPCOR service areas. Check your bill to confirm your provider, and ask it for the latest water quality report.
