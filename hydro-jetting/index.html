When a backed-up line calls for hydro jetting right away

Jetting is not for every clog. It is for the situations where a cable has already failed or will not last.

  • A cable stuck partway down the line and could not get past a blockage.
  • A drain that clears and comes back within weeks.
  • Main line backups affecting several fixtures at once.
  • Heavy grease from a kitchen or restaurant line.

What to do while you wait

Stop running water into the affected line and keep floor drains clear of belongings.

Do not pour chemical cleaners into a standing backup. They create a hazard for the person who has to work on the line.

What a cable leaves behind and a jet removes

A drum cable cuts a channel through a blockage. It does not scrape the full pipe wall, so grease, soap and mineral scale stay behind. The line looks open but has a smaller bore, and the next clog forms faster.

A jet nozzle sends water backward and forward at high pressure, which cuts the coating around the full circumference of the pipe. The result is a line closer to its original diameter.

Roots, grease and scale: three different problems

Each blockage responds to a different nozzle and pressure.

Why we look with a camera first

High-pressure water in a cracked, collapsed or badly offset pipe can make a bad pipe worse. A quick camera pass tells us whether the line is sound enough to jet, and it records what the blockage is so the right nozzle goes on.

It also protects you. If the camera shows a broken section, you hear about it before pressure is applied, and we shift to repair instead.

Hydro jetting for kitchens, laundry lines and small businesses

Kitchen drains that carry grease are the most common candidates. Restaurants and food service sites need a regular schedule because grease forms in the line between visits. Grease trap pumping and cleaning is its own service, and we explain what is involved on our commercial plumbing page.

For houses, a jet is typically used on the main line or a long branch, not on a simple sink trap. A bathroom sink hair clog does not need it.

What a jet cannot do

Hydro jetting cleans. It does not patch a crack, remove a collapsed section or straighten an offset joint. If the line has failed, we explain the repair path and do not repeat jetting that will not hold. A line that clogs again after jetting is an honest signal the pipe needs repair.

Code note: why we camera first

Model codes treat the building drain as a pressure-free gravity line, and old pipe can be fragile. We run a camera before jetting so we do not send high-pressure water into a pipe that cannot take it.
