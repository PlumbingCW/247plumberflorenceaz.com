Sewer backup: what to do first

A backup is not only unpleasant. Raw wastewater is a health risk, and it can ruin flooring within hours. If the lowest drain in the house is overflowing, treat it as an emergency.

What to do while you wait

Stop all water use, including flushing and laundry, and keep children and pets out of the area.

Do not mix chemical drain cleaners with wastewater. They are hazardous and rarely clear a blockage that is caused by a broken pipe.

If you can reach it safely, find the sewer cleanout, often a capped pipe in the yard near the house, and tell the plumber where it is. Photograph the damage for your insurance.

Signs your sewer line is failing

A failing sewer line gives warnings before it fails completely.

  • Slow drains in several fixtures at once, or toilets that gurgle when another fixture drains.
  • Recurring clogs that a cable clears for a few weeks and then return.
  • A sewer odor outside near the cleanout or inside near floor drains.
  • Green, soggy patches in the yard along the line, or a sunken strip of ground.
  • Cracks in the slab or foundation that appeared after repeated backups.

How we diagnose the line

We feed a camera on a flexible cable through a cleanout and record the pipe from the house to the street connection. It shows roots, offset joints, cracks, scale, a belly where water sits and any collapse.

A locator at the surface picks up the camera signal and tells us exactly where the problem sits and how deep it is. That precision matters because it decides whether the fix is a spot repair or a longer replacement.

You see the footage, and we explain what each defect means. The camera visit can be booked on its own through sewer inspection, or as the first step of this repair.

Pipe lining and spot repair for cracked lines

A common call is a localized crack with root intrusion in an otherwise decent pipe. When the camera shows exactly that, a spot repair or a short liner can fix the defect without replacing the whole line. We explain whether that works on your pipe or whether the defects are too many.

Lining uses a resin-soaked tube that is pulled into the cleaned pipe and cured to form a new inner wall. It needs a pipe that is open and mostly intact. If the camera shows a collapse, a major offset or a long bellied section, replacement is the honest answer.

Old cast iron, clay and septic questions

Older homes can have cast iron or clay sewer lines that crack, corrode or let roots in at joints. Cast iron pipe repair is handled with the same camera-first approach, and we describe the options for your line.

Some Florence-area properties outside town are on septic systems and not on a municipal sewer. If your home is on septic, say so when you call. A septic problem has a different cause and fix, and we tell you whether it is something we handle.

Call 811 before any digging

Arizona 811 says Arizona law requires you to contact it before any operation that moves ground with tools or equipment, and utility locators have two full working days to respond. Marks stay valid for 15 working days. We make the call for any dig on our jobs, so buried gas, power and water lines are marked first.

Source: Arizona 811.

Code note: slope, cleanouts and permits

The UPC and IPC set minimum slope for building sewers and require access for cleaning. Sewer replacement is usually permitted work, and the inspector may want to see the pipe before it is covered.
