About the Anthem at Merrill Ranch community

A community guide lists Anthem at Merrill Ranch at about 3,200 acres, with its main address at 3200 N Anthem Way, Florence, AZ 85132. It has two parts. Sun City Anthem is the age-restricted 55+ section, and Parkside is the all-ages section with two-story homes.

The same guide lists a 43,000 square foot community center, a water park, more than 60 acres of parkland and the Poston Butte Golf Club. Building has not stopped. A December 2025 Town planning update reported that the Town council had approved a final plat for Anthem at Merrill Ranch in the area east of Felix Road.

If you live in the 55+ section, see our page on plumbing in Sun City Anthem. For the wider area, see the Florence service area.

Sources: Private Communities guide to Anthem at Merrill Ranch, Homes.com neighborhood guide, and Town of Florence planning updates on Citizen Portal.

Two-story homes and where leaks travel

In a two-story house, a small leak upstairs does not stay upstairs. Water from a bathroom supply line, a tub drain or a washing machine hose can soak a ceiling and walls in the rooms below. That is general plumbing knowledge, not a claim about any one home.

What to do while you wait on a plumbing emergency

These steps limit damage before anyone arrives.

  • Close the main water valve if a pipe is leaking or burst. See burst pipe repair.
  • Turn off power to the water heater if the tank is leaking, and close its cold supply valve. See water heater repair.
  • Stop using toilets, tubs and sinks if sewage is backing up. See sewer line repair.
  • Photograph any damage for your insurance claim before cleanup.

Newer homes still need routine plumbing care

Homes built in recent years have newer pipe, but fixtures and appliances still wear out. A tank water heater has a limited life, so note its age and schedule a look before it fails. See water heater replacement when a tank is leaking or past its expected life.

Test the shutoff valves under sinks and toilets once a year so they still turn when you need them. Replace braided washing machine hoses on a schedule, and know where the main valve is before an emergency.

Permits and code in Anthem

Anthem sits inside the Town of Florence, so the Town Community Development Department at 224 W. 20th Street, 520-868-7575, is the office to ask about permits. Plumbing work follows the code the Town has adopted, usually based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) or the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Ask which edition applies before a water heater swap, a repipe or a sewer repair.
