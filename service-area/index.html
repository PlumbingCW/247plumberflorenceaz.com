Florence and the surrounding area

Florence is the Pinal County seat. We work in the Townsite, along Hunt Highway, on AZ-79 and AZ-287 and at Anthem at Merrill Ranch, a Pulte and Del Webb community about 6 miles northwest of downtown.

How water and permits differ across the area

Three different water suppliers serve the towns we cover, and that affects the advice we give. Florence Water, run by the Town, uses four groundwater wells. EPCOR serves Anthem at Merrill Ranch and parts of San Tan Valley. Arizona Water Company serves Coolidge.

Permits differ too. The Town of Florence Community Development Department at 224 W. 20th Street, 520-868-7575, handles addresses inside town limits. Pinal County Building Safety at 85 N Florence Street handles unincorporated areas and, from July 1, 2026, San Tan Valley under an agreement with the Town.

Each town page explains which utility and which office apply, so you know who to call about the pipes on each side of your meter.

Emergency calls across the area

Our dispatch line works the same way in every town. You describe the problem, we tell you what to shut off, and a plumber heads out. Travel time varies with distance and traffic, so ask the dispatcher for an honest estimate when you call.

  • Burst or leaking pipes with water you cannot stop.
  • Sewer backups into tubs, showers and floor drains.
  • Failed water heaters and leaking tanks.
  • Hidden leaks that show as a warm floor or a bill spike.

Roads and landmarks that help with directions

AZ-79 and AZ-287 are the main routes in and out of Florence. Hunt Highway runs past Poston Butte, which rises to 1,749 feet and has a pyramid tomb and a short trail. St. Anthony's Monastery, founded in 1995, is another well-known landmark in the area.

Giving the dispatcher a nearby cross street or landmark gets a plumber to the right door faster, especially in newer subdivisions where street names repeat.

Commercial and rental properties in the area

Landlords, property managers and business owners use the same dispatch line. Tell us the building type, the unit and who can open the door. We note what we find so the work order is easy to close, and we can work around business hours when the problem allows it.

Restaurants and shops often need grease trap service, floor drain clearing and backflow testing. Our commercial plumbing page lists what we handle and what we refer elsewhere.

Which plumbing code applies

Each town or the county applies its own adopted plumbing code, usually based on the UPC or IPC. The building department for your address says which edition applies and which jobs need a permit. This page was written by the Florence 24/7 Plumbing team.

What to tell the dispatcher

A short description saves time.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening and when it started.
  • Whether water is flowing and whether the main valve is closed.
  • Any access notes, such as gates or pets.
