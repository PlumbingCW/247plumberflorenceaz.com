When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most single-fixture clogs can wait a few hours. A few situations cannot, because wastewater is already coming into the house or the line has failed completely.

  • Sewage or gray water rising in a tub, shower or floor drain when you flush or run water.
  • Every fixture in the house draining slowly or gurgling at the same time.
  • Water standing in the kitchen sink with the disposal running and the dishwasher backed up.
  • A sewer smell inside the house along with any of the above.

If more than one fixture is affected, the blockage is probably in the main drain or the building sewer, and that is the call to make right away.

What to do while you wait

Stop running water in the house. Every flush and every load of laundry adds to what has nowhere to go.

Pull up rugs and move belongings away from the lowest drain, usually a shower or tub on the ground floor. Keep children and pets out of any wastewater and wash your hands after touching anything it reached.

What blocks drains in Florence homes

The Town of Florence says its water comes from groundwater wells, according to the 2025 Consumer Confidence Report from Florence Water. Groundwater in the Sonoran Desert carries dissolved minerals that can leave scale inside pipes over the years. That report does not list a hardness number, so ask Town Utilities for one or test your tap before assuming anything about your own lines.

Age matters as much as water. Buildings in the Florence Townsite Historic District, which the National Register listed in 1982, include structures well over a century old, so drain lines there may have been patched or rerouted more than once. A camera shows what is really underground.

Newer subdivisions such as Anthem at Merrill Ranch have the opposite pattern. The pipes are younger, and the clogs are usually grease, hair, wipes labeled flushable and building debris from the first years of the house.

Kitchen sink clogs and why they come back

A kitchen drain carries grease, food scraps and soap residue, and it runs through a trap and a long horizontal run where those materials cool and stick. The sink drains a little slower each week until one night it stops.

  • Standing water on one side of a double sink usually means a clog past the disposal or in the branch line.
  • Both sides full and the dishwasher backing into the sink points to the shared line downstream of the tee.
  • A sink that clears for a week and slows again means the cable made a hole through the clog but did not remove the coating on the pipe wall.

That last pattern is why we scrape the line wall with the right cutter head, or move up to hydro jetting, instead of only poking a hole. If the disposal itself is jammed, garbage disposal repair is a separate fix.

Snaking a drain without damaging the pipe

A cable machine is safe in a sound pipe when the operator matches cable size and cutter to the line. Problems start when someone forces a cable through a broken joint or past a cracked clay or cast iron section.

If the cable stops at the same spot twice, we pull it back and put a camera in. A blockage that will not move is often a pipe problem, and the answer is then sewer line repair instead of more cabling. A camera inspection can be booked on its own through sewer inspection when you only want to know the condition of the line.

Code note: traps and vents

The Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and the International Plumbing Code (IPC) both require a trap and a vent on each fixture drain. If a clearing visit exposes a missing or undersized vent, he tell you, because a vent fault can cause the same slow, gurgling drains that a clog does.
