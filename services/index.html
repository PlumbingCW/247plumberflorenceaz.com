Drains and sewer

Blocked drains and damaged sewer lines are the most common emergency calls. We clear, inspect and repair.

Drain Cleaning

Kitchen sinks, showers, tubs, laundry lines and main-line clogs cleared with cable machines and cameras.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water scouring for grease, scale, sludge and roots when a cable will not finish the job.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Clearing the main line from the house to the street, installing cleanouts and scheduling maintenance.

Sewer Line Repair

Camera diagnosis and repair of cracked, rooted, sagging or collapsed sewer lines, including pipe lining.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Video inspection of the sewer line with a locator, for repeat clogs, home purchases and repair planning.

Water heaters

Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. We repair, replace and install both.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, pilot and igniter faults, bad elements and thermostats, and leaking tanks and fittings.

Water Heater Replacement

Replacing a failed tank or an aging unit with a gas, electric, hybrid or tankless heater.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs for new construction, remodels and upgrades, with sizing, venting and code items covered.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless install, repair, error codes, and annual flush and descale service.

Leaks and supply lines

Leaks start small and grow. We find them, stop the damage and repair or replace the pipe.

Leak Detection

Pinpoint hidden leaks behind walls, under floors and underground, plus meter and high-bill checks.

Slab Leak Repair

Locating and repairing leaks in water lines under or inside a concrete slab, with minimal demolition.

Burst Pipe Repair

Cracked, split and burst supply and drain pipes in copper, PVC, PEX and galvanized, repaired fast.

Water Line Repair and Replacement

Leaks and failures in the underground line between the meter and the house, plus low water pressure.

Repiping

Replacing aging supply pipes in whole or in part when repairs keep adding up.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

These steps limit damage in almost any plumbing emergency.

  1. 1Stop the water.Close the main valve, and the fixture valve if the leak is at a sink or toilet.
  2. 2Make it safe.Keep people away from wet electrical outlets and wastewater. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911.
  3. 3Protect belongings.Move rugs, boxes and furniture off the wet floor.
  4. 4Document.Take photos for your insurance claim.
