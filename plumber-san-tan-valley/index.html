What changed in San Tan Valley in 2025 and 2026

San Tan Valley incorporated as a town on September 17, 2025. The 2020 census counted 99,894 people there, up about 22.8 percent from 81,321 in 2010. A community that large keeps adding homes and the plumbing that goes with them.

Pinal County announced that from July 1, 2026, it handles building safety, plan review, permitting and inspections for San Tan Valley under an agreement with the Town. For a permitted job such as a water heater or repipe, contact Pinal County Building Safety at 85 N Florence Street in Florence, phone 520-509-3555.

Hard water in the San Tan Valley area

EPCOR's water quality report for its San Tan system, which serves roughly 31,000 connections from groundwater, lists hardness of 15 to 18 grains per gallon. One grain per gallon is 17.1 mg/L, so that is about 257 to 308 mg/L, which the USGS calls very hard.

The report describes the system as a whole. Check your bill or ask EPCOR which system serves your address before applying that number to your own tap.

Which ZIP codes are San Tan Valley?

Four ZIP codes touch the area. ZIP 85140 is the largest by population among those that use the San Tan Valley name. ZIP 85142 lists Queen Creek as its primary city, with San Tan Valley as an accepted alias, and it spans Maricopa and Pinal counties. ZIPs 85143 and 85144 complete the set.

We dispatch to all four. If your address sits near a county line, give us the street and cross street, and the dispatcher will confirm coverage.

Older homes and new builds both need plumbing help

Homes built in the early growth years now have water heaters near the end of their life, supply lines that are aging and fixtures worn by scale. Newer homes bring a different set of calls: builder-grade fixtures, poor drain slopes and installation faults that show up under a camera.

A single question guides most visits: is this a repair or a replacement? We explain both so you can choose. See repiping and plumbing installation.

Code and permits in San Tan Valley
